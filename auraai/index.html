      How do I get a key? (free)
      1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
      2. Press “Create API key”, then copy it.
      3. Paste it above and press “Save and test”.

      Your messages are sent to Google's Gemini to write Aura's replies. Your key stays on this device. If you say you might hurt yourself, Aura never uses AI: it shows a fixed safety message with a helpline.

      What Aura knows about me

      Aura learns things from what you say in chat, like what you like or what helps you. You can see, change, or delete all of it. It stays on this device. If AI is on, it is shared with the AI so Aura can know you better.

      Aura does not keep messages about hurting yourself or others, swearing, phone numbers, addresses, or other people's names.
