// AI study tools

Ask, revise, generate

Tap any tool for detail.

HELP

Homework Helper

Ask anything, or photograph a worksheet.
Pick how the answer is written — step-by-step, check-my-working, exam technique, ELI5, or “why am I wrong?” Real walkthroughs, and it remembers the conversation for follow-ups.
STUDY

Smart Study

Any topic becomes a revision toolkit.
Flashcards, quizzes, practice questions, mark schemes, cloze, formula sheets, Cornell notes, mnemonics, predicted questions, common mistakes, comparisons. Export sets as PDF.
NOTES

Notes

Messy text becomes something study-able.
Paste a transcript or textbook page and get a summary, bullet points, mind map, essay plan, glossary, timeline, or a plain-language rewrite. Upload a photo instead of pasting.
EXAM

Exam Papers

Build a custom mock exam.
Choose the subject, question types, difficulty, number of questions and level. You get a real mock paper built to that exact spec — not a generic quiz.
LIB

Topic Library

Pre-built explainers by level.
Browse topics by KS3, GCSE, A-Level or University and subject. Tap one for an instant AI explainer — good for revision when you don't have a specific question yet.
MUSE

Muse

Describe an idea, get an image.
Photorealistic, anime, watercolour, sketch, minimalist, or diagram style. Diagram mode is genuinely useful for revision. Every image saves to your Vault automatically.
// Wellbeing & social

Study life, not just study

TALK

Companion

An AI to talk to about stress and study life.
Check in with a mood chip or just start typing. “Debate Me” makes it argue back to sharpen your reasoning.
PRIV

Journal

A private space, saved on your device.
Type or dictate a voice note. Filter and search past entries. Export any time. Not shown to anyone.
ASK

Forum

Students helping students, by subject.
Ask a question, share a tip, answer someone else's. Filter by subject. Google sign-in to post.
RANK

Leaderboard

Compete on gems, streaks or games.
Add friends by friend-code, or see where you land globally. Global visibility is off until you turn it on.
THE SAFETY NET If a message to the Companion suggests real distress or danger, Veda alerts a guardian — but it tells the student first, and always shows crisis resources in the chat. It's a safety net, not silent monitoring.
